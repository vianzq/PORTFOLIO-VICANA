I'm Kai Reyes, a third-year BS Computer Science student at the University of San Carlos in Cebu City, Philippines. I'm passionate about the intersection of technology, people, and meaningful design.

Growing up in Cebu — a city that blends heritage and hustle — shaped my appreciation for diverse perspectives. Now, I'm preparing to bring that perspective to the world stage through study abroad.

The Road So Far

My Journey

2021

University Begins

Enrolled in BS Computer Science at the University of San Carlos. Immediately fell in love with algorithms, design, and the potential of technology to solve real-world problems.

2022

First Research Paper

Co-authored a research paper on accessibility in mobile UI, presented at a regional student conference. Discovered a passion for human-centered technology.

2023

National Hackathon Winner

Led a team of four to first place at the National ICT Olympiad Hackathon — building a disaster-response coordination app for underserved communities in the Visayas region.

2024

Exchange Program Accepted

Selected for the ASEAN University Network exchange program and received a full scholarship from the DOST for graduate studies abroad. The next chapter begins.

2025 →

Study Abroad

Departing for NTU Singapore to pursue advanced coursework in Human-Computer Interaction and AI Ethics. Excited to grow, contribute, and return with the tools to make a difference.
